Write to support@neron.app. We read English and Turkish.

Common questions

  • A picture looks wrong or upsetting. Open it and tap Report. The report reaches us with the picture's job number; you don't need to send the photo.
  • I bought something on a new phone. Open Dreadcam and tap Restore purchases on the home screen. A weekly plan and the October pass restore; a single picture is used when it is bought.
  • Cancel the weekly plan. Apple: Settings > your name > Subscriptions. Google Play: Play Store > profile > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions.
  • Delete my data. Result files are deleted automatically 30 days after they are made. To have them and the related records deleted sooner, email support@neron.app; Delete your data has the details.

Dreadcam is made by Neron. Every result is an AI-generated image, made for fun.
