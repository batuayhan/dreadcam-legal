Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Dreadcam ("the App", "we") turns a photo you choose into an image that looks like security-camera footage with a blurred figure far behind you. This policy says what leaves your device, who receives it and why.

Key points: your photo is processed on our server and checked by fal.ai; we do not use your photos to train anything; there are no accounts; every result is labelled "AI-generated image".

1. Who can use the App

The App is for people aged 18 and over. Do not upload photos of children. We do not knowingly collect information about anyone under 18.

2. Your photo

  • You choose a photo with the system photo picker. The App can only see the photo you pick.
  • Each time before an upload the App asks for your consent. Without it, nothing is uploaded.
  • Face detection runs on your phone (Google ML Kit). Only the position boxes it finds are sent with the photo, not any face data.
  • The photo is sent to our server (Google Firebase, region europe-west1). The server sends it on to fal.ai, an AI service provider, for two things only: a safety check (moderation) and finding where people are in the photo so the added figure is placed behind them.
  • The image model is not given your photo and the figure is added by our own code. We do not use your photos to train any model, and we do not sell them.
  • Our server does not store the photo you upload. fal.ai receives it to answer the two requests above and keeps uploaded files under its own terms; see fal.ai/privacy.
  • Photos that fail the safety check are rejected and no result is made.

3. Results we store

For each picture we make, our server stores the finished result files (a preview, a still image and a short GIF). They are made from your photo, so they show what is in it. They are kept in Google Cloud Storage and can be opened only through time-limited private links. Result files are deleted automatically 30 days after they are created. The storage service's soft-delete protection is on, so a deleted file may stay recoverable by us for a limited time before it is permanently removed. The job record described in section 4 and any report (section 5) are not deleted automatically; we delete them on request. You can ask us to delete your files and records sooner at any time by e-mail (section 8).

4. Device identifier and abuse protection

There are no accounts. To enforce one free picture per device and to stop abuse, the App sends a device identifier to our server. The server stores it only as a salted, one-way hash, not in readable form. The same applies to the anonymous purchase ID used to count paid single pictures. Along with it we keep counts such as how many free pictures were used, and a record of each job (type of figure, time, cost, technical checks and the number of faces your phone found in the photo, as a count only).

5. Reports

You can report an image inside the App. A report stores the picture's job ID, the type of figure and the random seed of that picture, the reason you choose, an optional note (up to 500 characters), the hashed device identifier and the time, so we can review it.

6. Purchases

Paid options are sold through Google Play: a single picture, weekly unlimited, and unlimited until 31 October. Google Play processes the payment and we never see your payment card. RevenueCat manages purchase status and receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase information; see revenuecat.com/privacy. Our server asks RevenueCat whether a purchase exists so it can unlock your picture.

7. Analytics and crash reports

The App uses Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics. They record events such as first open, a picture being made, a paywall being shown, a purchase, plus technical device and app information and crash traces. They use a random app-instance identifier and not your name or e-mail, and Firebase derives an approximate location (country or region) from your IP address. See firebase.google.com/support/privacy. We do not use advertising SDKs. Separately, our server counts how many times shared links are opened per day, as a number only, with nothing about the visitor.

8. Your choices and deletion

To have your result files and related records deleted, e-mail support@neron.app and include the share link of the picture, or the job ID, if you have it; without it we usually cannot find results by device, because the stored identifier is hashed and the App does not show it to you. Depending on where you live (EEA/UK GDPR, California CCPA, Turkish KVKK No. 6698 or other laws) you may also ask about, correct or delete information we hold, and you may complain to your local data protection authority. Uninstalling the App does not delete result files on our server.

9. Security

Files are private and reachable only through time-limited links. The server key for the AI provider is never in the App. No system is perfectly secure.

10. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. Material changes will be shown in the App or on this page.
